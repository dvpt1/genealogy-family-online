About Me

In 2003, I became fascinated with researching my family tree. At first, these were simple notes on paper cards and photocopied documents, but very quickly it turned into a chaotic archive without structure or order. In 2005, I began searching for a ready-made program for maintaining a family tree, but, as often happens with programmers, none of the solutions satisfied me. Then I decided to write my own system.

This passion gradually grew into a full-fledged development project. I have created genealogy apps for various platforms: Windows, Android, Linux, macOS, and iOS. To make this possible, I had to constantly master new technologies and programming languages. As a result, by the age of 63, I am fluent in approximately 18 programming languages.

Over time, it became clear that even a good program couldn't solve the main problem. The problem wasn't data storage or information display, but rather the inability of relatives to conveniently share family materials with each other.

A family tree isn't just a personal archive, but a shared family history, made up of memories, photographs, documents, and stories from different people, often living in different cities and countries.
